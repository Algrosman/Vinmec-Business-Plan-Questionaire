Advanced Prototype

Welcome

This version expands the questionnaire and improves the output so it feels more like a strategic planning tool rather than a simple form. It is designed to support working sessions with Cleveland Clinic Connected and Vinmec stakeholders.

What it captures

  • Strategic purpose and ambition
  • Market and patient priorities
  • Clinical scope and service lines
  • Capacity and phasing assumptions
  • Operating model and workforce needs
  • Digital, quality, facilities, and finance inputs

What it generates

  • Executive summary
  • Business plan outline
  • Decision summary
  • Assumptions requiring validation
  • Open questions
  • Recommended next-step agenda

Suggested workshop use

  • Use one facilitator to enter responses live during stakeholder discussions.
  • Review the generated output after each major module.
  • Identify where the group has made a decision versus where assumptions remain untested.
  • Use the “open questions” and “next steps” sections to shape follow-up meetings.

A strategic planning tool to help define the vision, operating assumptions, clinical scope, and business plan outline for Vinmec’s future hospital in Can Gio.

Format: Single-file prototype

Consulting-style prototype

Welcome

This version is designed to support structured planning workshops between Cleveland Clinic Connected and Vinmec stakeholders. It captures strategic, clinical, operational, workforce, digital, facilities, and financial inputs, then translates them into a draft business plan output.

What this tool captures

  • Strategic purpose and ambition
  • Market demand and patient segments
  • Clinical scope and service-line choices
  • Capacity, phasing, and growth assumptions
  • Operating model and workforce strategy
  • Digital, quality, facilities, and finance inputs

What this tool generates

  • Executive summary
  • Business plan outline
  • Decision summary
  • Assumptions to validate
  • Open questions
  • Recommended next-step agenda
